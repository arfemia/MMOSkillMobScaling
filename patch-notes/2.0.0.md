Mob Scaling 2.0.0 runs with MMO Skill Tree 2.0 and CommonLib 3.0, and its admin page takes the new look every menu gets in this update.

Built for Hytale Update 7. It needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 3.0.0 and MMO Skill Tree 2.0.0 or newer.

## Changes

- The admin page (`/mobscaling ui`) has the new look: its five tabs sit along the top like the game's own header tabs, and the page is wider so they fit.
- The Fire and Frost dragons and the Scarak Broodmothers, which Mob Scaling makes bosses, count as bosses to every mod on your server, so MMO Skill Tree's boss rules apply to them too.
- A scaled monster's toughness counts against the flat damage MMO Skill Tree's talents and abilities add to a hit, the same as it does against the rest of the hit.
- The Restless Graveyard from Seasons of Orbis scales its creatures from level 1 to 10, like the Hallow's Eve graveyard.

## For server owners

- The demo caster roster's ice ball is Mob Scaling's own now, since MMO Skill Tree 2.0 no longer has Draconic Arcana. A roster of your own that names `"AbilityId": "dragon_arcana"` casts nothing and logs one warning: point it at `"NativeChain": "Mmoscaling_Demo_Ice_Ball"`. The demo ships switched off.
- If you override `Mmoscaling_Bosses`, keep `Zig_World_Bosses` under `IncludeGroups` unless you mean to drop the shipped bosses, and list your own under `IncludeRoles`.
