A hotfix cut from 1.2.4 that builds and runs on Hytale Update 8 (0.8.0-pre.5 and later 0.8 builds): `ServerVersion` is `>=0.8.0-pre.0 <0.9.0`. The pins follow the family in lockstep, `ziggfreedCommonVersion` 2.5.3 and `mmoSkillTreeVersion` 1.7.5; nothing here calls newer API, so the manifest floors stay `>=2.5.0` and `>=1.7.1`.

- **Zone floors on Update 8's generator.** `ZoneDifficultyResolver` reads `ChunkGenerator` and `ZoneBiomeResult` from `builtin.worldgen.chunk`, where Update 8 moved the generator its overworld still builds, with the same members. Without the move the resolver's guard would read no zone on Update 8, and every world would fall back to its baseline.
