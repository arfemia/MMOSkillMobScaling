Requires Ziggfreed Common 2.5.0 and MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1: the jar reads 2.5.0's `Scale.Companion` through `EncounterRuntime.companionScale` and `companionScaleOf`, so the manifest's `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon` floor moves from `>=2.3.0` to `>=2.5.0`; nothing here calls newer MMO API, so `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` stays `>=1.7.1`. The pins follow the family in lockstep: `ziggfreedCommonVersion` 2.5.0 and `mmoSkillTreeVersion` 1.8.0. One change: a boss whose binding row opts in is scaled with its fight.

- **A companion-scaled boss is scaled at the engage.** The roll still skips an encounter-bound boss. The one exception is a boss whose binding row sets ZiggfreedCommon's `Scale.Companion`: the new `event/EncounterSubjectScaling` listens for `EncounterEngagedEvent` and, for such a row, scales the bound boss on the world's next task with `MobScaleFold.anchored`, a plain fold at the adds' level (each axis divided by its value at the spot's effective floor, never below 1; in a dungeon copy that floor is the world rule's `Floor`, so a boss at the bottom of the band is unchanged and an open-world boss elsewhere never shrinks), with no rarity, affix, aura or caster kit. `Companion.Health` writes `mmoscaling_hp` as a first write (the bar only: the hidden damage reduction stays 1.0) and `Companion.Damage` the outgoing-damage bucket, stamped `ScaledMobComponent.ofCompanion`, which the caster arm skips. The owner exclusion and the hostile gate apply as to any mob. The scale is kept per run until `EncounterResetEvent`, and both roll skips put it back on a reloaded boss of an engaged fight instead of stripping it. One INFO line per scaled fight (`encounter boss scaled at the engage: ... health x..., hits x...`) is the smoke's evidence. The new path is `LinkageError`-guarded, the mis-install net the other encounter seams carry. No boss id lives in this mod: Seasons of Orbis 1.2.2's two Headless Horseman rows are the first to opt in. Test: `MobScaleFoldTest` (the anchored fold).
