- **MMO Skill Tree is no dependency** (SK14-a, R597): the mod builds and runs on ZiggfreedCommon alone, and meets the MMO only through ZiggfreedCommon's seams. The manifest's `Dependencies` names ZiggfreedCommon alone (`Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` out), `build.gradle` drops the MMO jar from `compileOnly` and `testImplementation` (and its path and coordinate), `gradle.properties` drops the `mmoSkillTreeVersion` pin, and the workspace's `family.properties` `dep.mmo-mob-scaling` (with `tools/wt.ps1`'s fallback) names `ziggfreed-common` alone. The release seam owes the ZiggfreedCommon floor: every seam below is new in CommonLib 3.0.0.
  - Player power is ZiggfreedCommon's player power seam (`config/PlayerPowerReads`, replacing `config/MmoPowerBounds`): the presence system, the zone HUD's own-power line, `/mobscaling inspect`, the boot caps cross-check and the admin page's read-only panel all read it. With the MMO it is the MMO's Power Level on its `Clamp`; without it, CommonLib's reading of worn gear (`PlayerPower` record, the owner menu's Player Power screen). A `BootEvent` INFO line names whose reading the scaling reads and on what scale.
  - The rarity attribution is a CommonLib `KillQualifier` (`MobScalingRarityAttribution`), contributed through `ProgressionRuntime.registrar("MmoMobScaling")`, so a criterion naming a rarity matches on a server without the MMO too. The mod contributes no `KillRewardScale` (see "Kill XP follows a mob's health" below).
  - A caster roster's `AbilityId` entry casts through CommonLib's `NpcAbilityCasts` (the MMO fills it); with no mod filling it, the entries are skipped for the session with one warning (`CasterFeatureState`), and `NativeChain` entries keep working.
  - The two HUD cards' per-player show is CommonLib's `PlayerSettings` under each card's `SURFACE` (`Mmoscaling_Zone_Difficulty`, `Mmoscaling_Mob_Inspector`), which the MMO's `/mmohud` and Settings switches write.
  - The damage filter orders itself before CommonLib's route step (`TypedDamageBonusSystem`), which every consumer fold adding to a hit's sum follows, in place of the MMO's `CombatDamageEventSystem`.
  - `ManifestTargetTest`: the MMO floor case is deleted with the dependency; `mmoSkillTreeIsNoDependency` holds the manifest and the test classpath free of the MMO.
- **The incoming multiply is recorded on the hit** (`event/MobScalingDamageFilter.recordToughness`): after it scales a hit on a scaled creature by its `inDmgMult`, the filter records that factor through ZiggfreedCommon's `NativeOutgoing.scaleHit`, so a flat a later step adds (the MMO's talent and ability flats, which join the hit after this filter) meets the same toughness rather than landing whole. The call is `LinkageError`-guarded, the mis-install net: on an older ZiggfreedCommon the multiply stands and only those flats land unscaled.
- **`Mmoscaling_Bosses` includes `Zig_World_Bosses` instead of its own `IncludeRoles`**: the roaming bosses the boss tier forces (the Fire and Frost dragons and the Scarak Broodmothers) are ZiggfreedCommon's group, which its `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite` includes too, so a boss this mod forces is a boss to every boss rule. An owner's override of the file keeps `Zig_World_Bosses` under `IncludeGroups` unless it means to drop the shipped ones, and lists its own roles under `IncludeRoles`.
- **The demo roster's ice ball is a native chain of this mod**, `Mmoscaling_Demo_Ice_Ball` (`Server/Item/RootInteractions/MmoMobScaling/`), no longer the MMO ability `dragon_arcana`, which MMO Skill Tree 2.0.0 stops shipping. `Demo_Boss_Caster`'s third entry names it as a `NativeChain`. The root runs the game's own `Goblin_Duke_Magic_1` interaction with the game's root's 2 second `Cooldown` and `Attack` `Ranged` tag, so it hits as the Goblin Duke's ice ball does (Physical, with its knockback). Like the dodge it arms once at spawn and fires only when the mob's own AI attacks, taking turns with the other armed chains in place of its usual attack. A roster that still names `"AbilityId": "dragon_arcana"` gets the caster's one-time "cast rejected" warning and casts nothing; point it at `"NativeChain": "Mmoscaling_Demo_Ice_Ball"` instead.
- **The place sets a mob's numbers, the group sets how rare it is** (R478, R1198; `scaling/SpawnDifficulty`): the spawn resolve returns two difficulties. The PLACE is the escalated floor (zone or biome floor plus the distance bonus, capped). The ROLL is the place plus the region's band-clamped group delta through CommonLib's `ScalingEngine` (held at or above the place under `OnlyRaiseDifficulty`), as the one difficulty was before. `MobScalingSpawnHook.SpawnScaling.difficulty()` is now that record (`place`, `stat`, `roll`) in place of a double. The rarity, variant and affix picks (and their `MinDifficulty` bands) read the roll; `MobScaleFold.fold(..., SpawnDifficulty, ...)` reads the curve at the STAT difficulty, `place + share * (roll - place)`, the share being the new `OpenWorld.GroupDeltaStatShare` (0 to 1, shipped 0 in `Settings/Default.json`; 1 is the single resolve again; between blends; every world file may set its own). `MobScaleResult` carries `difficulty` (the stat difficulty: what the HUD's tier word, the inspector and the `mob_difficulty` factor read) and the new `rollDifficulty` (what `MobScalingCasterArmSystem` gates a roster entry's `MinDifficulty` on). The double `fold` and `plain` overloads stay for a caller with no group on top (`SpawnDifficulty.placeOnly`). An encounter boss's companion scale (`event/EncounterSubjectScaling`) reads `anchored(stat, place)`, so at share 0 he is exactly as his fight authors him. SK14-c's `MobScalingEngageSystem.watchFor` stays right after the roll's stamp, and the roll's `reconcileMaxHealth(result.hpMult())` stays the place's health; the party joins through `scaled.healthMult()` as before. No player power is read anywhere but CommonLib's player power seam.
- **`OpenWorld.GroupDeltaStatShare` on the admin page**: the Global tab's open world group and the Worlds tab each carry it as a 0 to 1 field ("Group share of health and damage"), the world field's hint naming what it inherits. The Global tab's preview column gains "What a strong group changes": at the ladder's difficulty, what a group with power at least the place plus the band rolls at against what health and damage read (`ScalingPreview.strongGroup`, the spawn's own resolve, from the form's band, caps and share; one line when player scaling is off). The read-only player power panel adds one line where a mod measures power: Mastery Pack bonuses count by what they do, weighted on MMO Skill Tree's Power Level page.
- **The HUD and `/mobscaling inspect` read both numbers.** The zone card's difficulty row is the place ("Difficulty 45", or "Difficulty 45, group +13" where the share lets the group reach health and damage), its tier word reads the stat difficulty against the viewer's power, and its group row says what the group adds to the roll ("Group power 85: rarer mobs +25"; "Group power: 85" where it adds nothing). The inspector card keeps "Difficulty N" (the stat difficulty) and adds a roll line under its header, `#MmoscalingInspectRoll` ("Rarity roll: 65 (players near)"), shown only when the mob's roll rounds above it; the card's height counts it (`MobInspectorHud.ROLL_LINE_PX`). `/mobscaling inspect` replaces its one "Effective spawn difficulty" line with the place line (or the place and the stat difficulty with the share's percent) and the roll line with what the group adds; its curve line reads the stat difficulty.
- **Kill XP follows a mob's health** (R610): the mod contributes no CommonLib `KillRewardScale`; `event/MobScalingXpReward` is deleted, and with it the rarity and variant `Multipliers.Xp` leaf (`RarityAsset`, `VariantAsset`, `Rarity.xpMult`, `Variant.xpMult`, `MobScaleResult.xpMult`, the fold's product, the validator's check and the five shipped files' values). A pack file still writing `Xp` gets the server's own "Unused key(s)" warning. A Legendary kill paid its payer's base kill XP times `Xp` 1.6 plus an underdog bonus of 0.01 a point its spot difficulty stood above the killer's power (at most 0.5), capped at 3.0: 1.6 to 2.1 times. It now pays what its maximum health pays where the payer reads it (CommonLib track sources' `PerMaxHealth`, under their caps); on the shipped curve a Legendary carries 1.66 times a plain mob's health at a place of 25, 1.71 at 30, 1.82 at 50 and 1.94 at 100. The MMO's kill payer reads its per-mob table until it moves onto CommonLib's kinds (SK10-b, SK10-d); until then a Legendary pays what a plain mob of its kind pays. The channel itself stays in CommonLib as a general tool.
- **Mobs count the party in front of them** (`Settings` `Party`, every leaf per world too): the first time a scaled mob's `LockedTarget` is a player, `event/MobScalingEngageSystem` counts the players within `Party.Radius` (40) once, through CommonLib's `Nearby.playersAround` with `scaling/PartyRule.partySize` deciding the edge (a player exactly at the radius counts), as the game's goblin dungeon does (`Component_Instruction_Dungeon_Goblin_Scale_Health`). Exactly two, exactly three, four or more multiply its health by `TwoPlayers`, `ThreePlayers`, `FourPlayers` (1.55, 2.1, 2.65); a boss or an elite (CommonLib's `BossRules.isBossOrElite`: `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite` or a `ZigRank` tag) adds `BossPerPlayer` (0.2, the Gobliterator's 1000 on 5000) of its health per player past the first, up to `BossMostPlayers` (4). The multiplier joins the place's in the one `mmoscaling_hp` reconcile (`ScaledMobComponent.healthMult()`, the party kept on the component as `partySize` and `partyHealthMult`) and the mob refills to its new maximum. No player stat is read. `Party.SkipGroups` (`Dungeon_Goblin_Pack`) names groups that grow with the party by themselves, so a party never pays twice; the four Shifting Deep world files set `"Party": { "Enabled": false }`, since a CommonLib dungeon already brings more foes for a bigger party. The Global and Worlds tabs of `/mobscaling ui` edit every leaf (the switch is an instant toggle, a tri-state per world).
- **The Rallying affix** (`Affixes/Rallying.json`, `Mmoscaling_Rally_Cry`, `Mmoscaling_Rallied`): the game's goblin scrapper's pack enrage on any scaled mob. An affix asset's new `Cry` group (`affix/AffixCry`: `WindupSeconds`, `CastEffectId`, `SoundEventId`, `BuffEffectId`, `BuffSeconds`, `Radius`, `RepeatMinSeconds`, `RepeatMaxSeconds`) makes it a war cry: once its mob has a target it wears the telegraph for the wind-up with the sound where it stands, then puts the buff for `BuffSeconds` on itself and on every creature within `Radius` hostile to its target (CommonLib's `Nearby.foesAround` of that target), and cries again a drawn wait later; a mob already wearing the buff holds its own cry. A pack adds another war cry by shipping an affix with a `Cry` group; its behaviour is that group, so it needs no `BehaviorId`, and the content audit checks its values and both effect ids. The buff's damage is an `OutgoingDamage` percent, so it adds to a scaled mob's own outgoing bonus as every damage effect does.
- **An owner layer over affixes** (`config/AffixOwnerLayer`, `config/AffixAssetConfig`): `mods/MmoMobScaling/affixes.json`, id-keyed, read through CommonLib's `OwnerLayerReader` against the pack's affix leaf by leaf (every `AffixAsset` leaf, the `Roll`, `FoldDeltas` and `Cry` leaves included, is now inherited, so a pack file's `Parent` works too), registered as the owner file `MmoMobScaling/affixes.json`. The CommonLib owner form `Mmoscaling_Affix_Cries` (War cries, `/zigadmin`, in this mod's own menu section `mmomobscaling`) edits each war-cry affix's weight, lowest difficulty and every `Cry` leaf. `AffixConfig` stays the runtime records, now derived from the folded assets.
- **The admin page (`/mobscaling ui`) takes CommonLib's Chapter look.** It sits on `@ZigDecoratedFrame`, the band, now 1000 wide so its five tabs fit: the tabs are CommonLib's view tabs, the game's header tabs, appended into `#MmoscalingTabBar` by `ViewTabPainter` and repainted on every switch (the switch's update carries their bindings, and a click naming no known tab is answered too), in place of the five `@RichBtn` tab buttons `SettingsUiUtil.setTabActive` tinted. `MmoscalingAdminPage.ui`, `MmoscalingWorldRow.ui` and `MmoscalingPreviewRow.ui` spell no colour: the hand-built rows, headers and dropdown take the settings form's own styles (`Common/ZigForm.ui`), the world row the kit's row surface, and every other word a CommonLib text style or token; the editor hint's "Inherits" segment and the preview's rail marker read `ZigTokens.INK_BRIGHT` and `TONE_AVAILABLE_TEXT`. Every name it uses is in CommonLib 2.5.0, so the manifest floor stays.
