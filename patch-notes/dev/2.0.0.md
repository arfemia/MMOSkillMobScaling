- **The incoming multiply is recorded on the hit** (`event/MobScalingDamageFilter.recordToughness`): after it scales a hit on a scaled creature by its `inDmgMult`, the filter records that factor through ZiggfreedCommon's `NativeOutgoing.scaleHit`, so a flat a later step adds (the MMO's talent and ability flats, which join the hit after this filter) meets the same toughness rather than landing whole. The call is `LinkageError`-guarded, the mis-install net: on an older ZiggfreedCommon the multiply stands and only those flats land unscaled.
- **`Mmoscaling_Bosses` includes `Zig_World_Bosses` instead of its own `IncludeRoles`**: the roaming bosses the boss tier forces (the Fire and Frost dragons and the Scarak Broodmothers) are ZiggfreedCommon's group, which its `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite` includes too, so a boss this mod forces is a boss to every boss rule. An owner's override of the file keeps `Zig_World_Bosses` under `IncludeGroups` unless it means to drop the shipped ones, and lists its own roles under `IncludeRoles`.
- **The demo roster's ice ball is a native chain of this mod**, `Mmoscaling_Demo_Ice_Ball` (`Server/Item/RootInteractions/MmoMobScaling/`), no longer the MMO ability `dragon_arcana`, which MMO Skill Tree 2.0.0 stops shipping. `Demo_Boss_Caster`'s third entry names it as a `NativeChain`. The root runs the game's own `Goblin_Duke_Magic_1` interaction with the game's root's 2 second `Cooldown` and `Attack` `Ranged` tag, so it hits as the Goblin Duke's ice ball does (Physical, with its knockback). Like the dodge it arms once at spawn and fires only when the mob's own AI attacks, taking turns with the other armed chains in place of its usual attack. A roster that still names `"AbilityId": "dragon_arcana"` gets the caster's one-time "cast rejected" warning and casts nothing; point it at `"NativeChain": "Mmoscaling_Demo_Ice_Ball"` instead.
- **The admin page (`/mobscaling ui`) takes CommonLib's Chapter look.** It sits on `@ZigDecoratedFrame`, the band, now 1000 wide so its five tabs fit: the tabs are CommonLib's view tabs, the game's header tabs, appended into `#MmoscalingTabBar` by `ViewTabPainter` and repainted on every switch (the switch's update carries their bindings, and a click naming no known tab is answered too), in place of the five `@RichBtn` tab buttons `SettingsUiUtil.setTabActive` tinted. `MmoscalingAdminPage.ui`, `MmoscalingWorldRow.ui` and `MmoscalingPreviewRow.ui` spell no colour: the hand-built rows, headers and dropdown take the settings form's own styles (`Common/ZigForm.ui`), the world row the kit's row surface, and every other word a CommonLib text style or token; the editor hint's "Inherits" segment and the preview's rail marker read `ZigTokens.INK_BRIGHT` and `TONE_AVAILABLE_TEXT`. Every name it uses is in CommonLib 2.5.0, so the manifest floor stays.
