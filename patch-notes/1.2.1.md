Two things made scaled mobs read wrong, at opposite ends: a mob could stop taking damage from a weapon entirely, and the far zones never got any harder. Both are fixed, along with what made them hard to diagnose. Underneath, every scaled mob's numbers are retuned around a player the mobs can hurt, so mob numbers change on every server. Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.2.0 and MMO Skill Tree 1.7.0, which ship alongside it.

### The big fixes

- **Fixed: a scaled mob could be immune to a weapon.** One player killed it in the usual number of hits. Another swung forever and the health bar never moved.
  - Damage lands as a whole number, so a hit cut small enough arrives as zero and still counts as a hit. A tanky mob, its armor and a resistance affix together could hold an ordinary weapon under that line for good.
  - This mod's own reduction no longer takes a landing hit down to nothing. The mob's armor and resistances still apply afterwards, as before.
- **Fixed: the far zones stayed low level.** Distance escalation started 15,000 blocks from spawn, further than players travel, so it never added anything. A Zone 4 spawn got only its zone's own difficulty, too far under the Legendary band for that tier to appear.
  - It starts at 5,000 blocks now, the edge of the protected ring around spawn: leave the ring and group scaling and the distance ramp begin together. A value you set yourself is untouched.
- **New: the mob inspector shows what the scaling did.** Beside the difficulty it rolled at, the crosshair panel gives the mob's health, the share of a hit it takes and how hard it hits, each as a percentage. A tough mob and a mob nothing can hurt used to look the same.

### The difficulty curve

- **Mobs are less spongy and hit harder, on purpose.** A plain mob at difficulty N is tuned as an even fight for a character at power N.
  - Health comes down hard: a top-difficulty mob was about two and a half times tougher to kill than an even fight wants. Most of the toughness shows on the health bar, with a little kept as quiet damage reduction, and the two multiply back to the same time to kill.
  - Damage goes up a great deal. It grew too slowly and capped out long before difficulty did, so a well-defended character at the top could stand still and take almost nothing. A character's defense grows about five times faster than its damage over a full progression, so a fair mob at the top has to hit much harder. The extra per point starts small and grows toward the top, so a new character meets a mob that hits only a little harder than a plain one.
  - Both ceilings sit well above an even fight, with a Boss at the very top of the scale just under them, so they stop runaway numbers without flattening the rarity ladder.
  - Casual, Hardcore and Playtest are three quarters, 1.4 times and 1.5 times the new curve.
  - Tuning by hand: `OutDamagePerPoint` is now `OutDamageScale` with `OutDamageShape`. At a shape of 1.0 the scale is exactly the old per-point slope; the shipped shape is 1.4.
  - Needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.0 and its lower defense caps: two halves of one change, and either alone will feel wrong.
- **A Legendary is a Legendary all the way up.** At difficulty 30 it has the health and damage of a plain mob at 72, and it keeps its lead over an Epic at every difficulty.
  - Rarities used to be health and damage multipliers that all hit the same ceiling at higher difficulties, so a Rare and a Legendary became the same tank with different names. A rarity is now one number: how much further along the curve the mob is read.
  - Rare, Epic, Legendary and Boss are about a third more, three quarters more, well over twice and three times as dangerous as a plain mob at the same spot, and the gap widens a little further out. The Horrific spider overlay adds a quarter on top of whichever tier it lands on.
  - Your own rarities: `Hp`, `OutDamage` and `InDamage` are gone, so move each tier's strength onto its `DifficultyMultiplier`. A file with the old keys still loads, and the start-up log names each one as an unused key, with its file.
- **The top of the difficulty scale is about 20,000 blocks from spawn, down from about 101,000.** Distance escalation adds a point every 87 blocks, where it was every 500, so walking out past the last shipped zone reaches the top of the scale. Owner overrides are untouched.
- **The word on the zone card means the same thing at every level.** It came from the gap between the zone's difficulty and your power, and fifteen points is a very different fight at power 10 than at power 100. Now it compares how hard a mob here hits with one matched to you, off the curve the mobs use, so the card and the mob always agree.
  - Fair means you would last within a quarter of a matched fight either way. Hard and Easy run out to two and a half times faster or slower; Deadly and Trivial are past that.
  - Some zones change label, mostly those far above or below your power. The difficulty number on the card is unchanged.

### Settings that are gone

- **The `Intensity` setting is gone, and so is `/mobscaling intensity`.** It scaled the whole curve at once and hid the real numbers, and the health keys it scaled no longer exist, so nothing converts it. Your files are reported, never rewritten.
  - At every start until you edit it, the log gives one line per file still setting `Intensity` or a retired curve key: your settings file, one of your world files, or a pack's settings file (a pack's world file only while the mod is switched on). The line gives the value found, the keys that replaced it with what your file reads for them now, each retired key with its value, and a suggested starting point for the damage side only.
- **Four settings that did nothing are gone.** `PresetMode`, and `AllowDifficultyIncreaseOnPartyJoin`, `LateArrivalBumpFactor` and `CompositionEnabled` under `OpenWorld`, were read in and never used. They leave the settings, the admin panel and the reference file. A config that still has them keeps working; the keys are ignored.

### More fixes

- **Fixed: a world with group scaling switched off reported zero power to a boss fight.** It reports nothing now, so the fight keeps its own posture. That holds for any world whose `AggregationMode` is `DISABLED`, before anyone has set foot in it as well as after, and for the `mmomobscaling:region_power` reading other mods' content can use.
- **Fixed: a world's own group-power setting is used.** A world that asked for the strongest nearby player (or the weighted mode) to set its difficulty got the average, because every world read a number already combined under the global setting. Each world uses its own choice now, and a change applies without anyone having to move.
- **Fixed: the Armored affix did nothing against most melee.** Resistance matches the exact kind of damage a hit deals, and a general kind does not cover the ones under it, so naming only the general kinds missed every swing that arrives as a crushing or cutting blow. It names those two as well now. The six elemental wards were already correct.
- **Fixed: a world or floor file you named with capital letters is the one the mod edits.** On a Linux server, saving a world from the admin panel whose file you had named `Arena.json` wrote a second `arena.json` beside it, and removing the rule missed the original.
  - Files are found by name ignoring case, on every read, save and delete, and a name the mod would spell differently, such as `Arena Big.json`, is read and saved as that file with no second `arena_big.json`. If a folder holds two files for one rule, the log names both.
- **Fixed: a world with no zone or biome name prefix lost that choice on a save in the admin panel.** The empty prefix came back from the Worlds tab as "inherit" and switched the world to the default names.
- **Fixed: an item, loot-table or stamped-item reward in a rarity's or variant's loot paid nothing, on every kill.** It reaches the player who made the kill now. Item drops, drop tables and commands in the same loot were never affected, and the shipped rarities and variants use none of these rewards, so this matters only if you added your own.
- **Fixed: the start-up log told you to match your difficulty ceiling to the MMO's player-power ceiling.** Setting it higher is fine, and it was never capped. Player power stops at its own maximum, so a higher difficulty ceiling is the only way for zone difficulty and distance escalation to out-scale a fully geared group.
  - The warning shows only when the ceiling is set too low now, where it flattens every strong group onto one difficulty, and it points at the setting that limits how hard those mobs hit.
- **Fixed: a missing companion took the whole mod down.** Only the damage filter is skipped now, with a line in the log saying so. The mobs keep their health, their loot and their abilities, and `/mobscaling purge` keeps working.
- **Fixed: the zone card showed every player a raw biome id under the zone's name**, such as "Ocean1". Hytale has no names for its biomes, so the card shows the zone's own name alone now. To show biomes, set a biome name key prefix and add a key for every biome; a blank prefix no longer prints the raw id.
- **Bosses keep their own name in death messages and the kill feed.** An Ember Dragon read "Boss Ember Dragon" there; it reads "Ember Dragon" now, and the mob inspector still tags it Boss. New: a rarity file of your own can do the same with `"DecorateName": false`. Every other tier still goes in front of the name, as in "Epic Skeleton".
- **The Ember Dragon's example spells are switched off.** An example file armed every Ember Dragon in the open world, on every server, with Fireball, an ice ball and a dodge. The file still ships as a model for your own. To turn it back on, copy `Server/MmoMobScaling/CasterRosters/Demo_Boss_Caster.json` out of the jar into your pack at the same path and set `"Enabled": true`. New: `"Enabled": false` switches off any caster roster.
- **The `Mmoscaling_Bosses` group drops the Void Guardian Golem**, which nothing in the game spawns, so no fight changes.
- **Arcaneward is now Spellward.** The affix descriptions in the language files say plainly what each affix does too: the six elemental wards shared one ornate line and now read "Takes less damage from fire." and the like, and Venomous no longer promises a burning venom it never had.

### Admin and tools

- **New: the admin panel edits the floors and shows you the fight.** A Floors tab lists every zone and biome floor, shipped or your own, and edits one in place; a blank field keeps the shipped value.
  - The Worlds editor can target a world by its gameplay config key and exclude worlds by pattern, which is how you reach an instance world whose name changes every time it is created.
  - The Global tab's preview gives each sample row the skeleton's real health, its real hit once the mod has seen one, and how many times longer it takes to kill than an unscaled mob. An amber note marks a row a ceiling is holding down, where raising a slope changes nothing.
  - Below the samples, a rarity ladder shows what Plain, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Boss come to at any difficulty you type, and a read-only panel shows the player power range the MMO hands this mod, how your minimum and maximum sit against it, and where the MMO edits those numbers.
- **New: retune a zone or biome floor from your own folder.** Drop a file named after a shipped mapping into `mods/MmoMobScaling/difficulty/`, with only the `Floor` you want in it, and that zone starts from your number. Delete the file and the shipped floor is back. A `README.txt` in the folder shows the format, and the start-up log names the folder.
- **New: you can say where distance escalation is measured from.** Set `Origin` under `DistanceEscalation` (an X and a Z in blocks; a minus sign is fine) in your settings file or a world file, or use the two new fields on the admin panel's Global tab and Worlds editor. Leave an axis blank and it follows the spawn point. The protected ring stays centred on spawn either way.
- **New: a world can place its own HUD overlays and size its own regions.** A world file accepted these keys before and ignored them. It can set where the zone card and the mob inspector sit (corner and pixel offsets), whether the zone card names the zone and biome and which name keys it uses, how far the inspector reaches and whether it shows a portrait, and how large a group-power region is. The admin panel's Worlds tab has a row for each.
- **New: `/mobscaling purge` runs from the server console.** A player still sweeps the world they stand in; the console sweeps every loaded world and reports each one in the log. It is the command for taking the mod off a server, so you no longer have to log in to run it.
- **The settings file says how distance is measured.** In blocks, as it always was, from the centre of the chunk a mob spawned in, so the reading moves 32 blocks at a time. That only shows if you test a few steps apart. The file also says escalation adds on top of the zone's difficulty, and that `/mobscaling inspect` tells you the distance where you stand, so you can pick a number without guessing.
- **Bigger text on the admin panel.** Row labels and buttons are a size up, and the notes and preview lines are easier to read. On the mob inspector, the rarity and level lines grew a little too.
