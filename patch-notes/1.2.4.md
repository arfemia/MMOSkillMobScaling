The Headless Horseman from Seasons of Orbis now scales with his graveyard's level, like the mobs around him.

MMO Mob Scaling 1.2.4 runs on Hytale Update 7 (0.7.x) with Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.0 or newer, up from 2.3.0, and MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 or newer.

### Changes

- With Seasons of Orbis 1.3.0, the Headless Horseman now scales with his graveyard's level like the mobs around him, both his health and how hard he hits, on both rides. A group at the bottom of the graveyard's band meets him exactly as tough as before.
