Mob Scaling 1.2.4 scales the Headless Horseman from Seasons of Orbis with his graveyard's level, like the mobs around him.

MMO Mob Scaling 1.2.4 runs on Hytale Update 7 (0.7.x) with Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.0 or newer and MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 or newer.

## New

- Added scaling for the Headless Horseman in Seasons of Orbis 1.2.2: he grows with his graveyard's level, both his health and how hard he hits, on both rides. A group at the graveyard's lowest level meets him exactly as tough as before.
